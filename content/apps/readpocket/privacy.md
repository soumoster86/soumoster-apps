ReadPocket is an Android app by SouMoster for reading, marking up and signing PDFs. This is the privacy policy for the app. The website has its own policy.

## What stays on the phone

PDFs you open, the page you stopped on, highlights, notes and filled forms stay on the device, unless you use the AI features described below. SouMoster does not offer an account, and the app does not show ads. The app has the internet permission only for those AI features. Without a key, it does not connect to anything.

## AI features

AI features are optional and stay off until you paste your own OpenAI API key in Settings. The key is encrypted on the phone, is sent only to OpenAI, and is left out of Android backups and device-to-device transfer. When you tap an AI action, the text it needs goes to OpenAI: the selected text, the current page, or the PDF's text up to a limit. For a question, that is your question and up to six best-matching pages. The PDF file itself, your annotations and your other documents do not. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background. The text goes directly from your phone to OpenAI over an encrypted connection, and OpenAI handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Usage is billed to your OpenAI account. SouMoster never receives your key or the text. To stop, remove the key in Settings.

## Backups and sharing

An encrypted backup is a file you keep. It is not stored in a SouMoster account. You can also open a PDF that another app shares in, or share a file out yourself.

## App lock and speech

If you turn app lock on, ReadPocket asks the phone to check your fingerprint or PIN. That check stays on the device. If you use read-aloud, the phone's own speech service speaks the text.
