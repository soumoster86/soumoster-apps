ScanPocket is the Pocket app for documents, text and QR codes. Scan a page into a PDF, read the words on it, make a QR code, and sign a page.

The library is on the phone. There is no SouMoster account, and there are no ads. Sharing a file uses the Android share sheet, which you start yourself. If you add your own OpenAI key, you can also summarise a scan or find its key details. That stays off until you do.

The app is still in development, so this page does not offer a Google Play button until a listing is live.
