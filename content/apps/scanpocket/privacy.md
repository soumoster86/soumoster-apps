ScanPocket is an Android app by SouMoster. It scans documents, reads text on a page, and works with QR codes. This is the privacy policy for the app. The website has its own policy.

## What stays on the phone

Pages, PDFs, recognised text and QR codes are stored in a private library on the device, unless you use the AI features described below. SouMoster does not offer an account, and the app does not show ads.

## Internet permission

ScanPocket requests internet access for the optional AI features below. A Google Play services library used for the on-device scanner also adds this permission. SouMoster does not run a server that receives your documents, and it does not offer an account.

## AI features

AI features are optional and stay off until you paste your own OpenAI API key in Settings. The key is encrypted on the phone, is sent only to OpenAI, and is left out of Android backups and device-to-device transfer. When you tap Summarise, Find key details or Suggest a name, the recognised text of that document goes to OpenAI. Page images and your other documents do not. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background. The text goes directly from your phone to OpenAI over an encrypted connection, and OpenAI handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Usage is billed to your OpenAI account. SouMoster never receives your key or the text. To stop, remove the key in Settings.

## App lock

If you turn app lock on, ScanPocket asks the phone to check your fingerprint or PIN. That check stays on the device.

## Sharing

You can send a PDF or image out through the Android share sheet. That share is something you start. Uninstalling the app removes its library from the phone.
