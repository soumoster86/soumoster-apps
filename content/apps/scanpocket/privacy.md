ScanPocket is an Android app by SouMoster. It scans documents, reads text on a page, and works with QR codes. This is the privacy policy for the app. The website has its own policy.

## What stays on the phone

Pages, PDFs, recognised text and QR codes are stored in a private library on the device. SouMoster does not offer an account, and the app does not show ads.

## Internet permission

ScanPocket's own manifest does not request internet access. The current release build still includes the internet permission, because a Google Play services library used for the on-device scanner merges it in. SouMoster does not run a server that receives your documents, and it does not offer an account.

## App lock

If you turn app lock on, ScanPocket asks the phone to check your fingerprint or PIN. That check stays on the device.

## Sharing

You can send a PDF or image out through the Android share sheet. That share is something you start. Uninstalling the app removes its library from the phone.
