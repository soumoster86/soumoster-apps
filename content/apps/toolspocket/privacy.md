ToolsPocket is an Android app by SouMoster with everyday tools for measuring, calculating, converting and reading your phone's sensors. This is the privacy policy for the app. The website has its own policy.

## What stays on the phone

Saved readings, the calculator tape, favourites, calibrations, timers, sensor recordings and preferences are all stored on your device. SouMoster does not offer an account, and the app does not show ads, use analytics or send crash reports. SouMoster never receives your data. If you use the AI features below, what each one says it sends goes to OpenAI.

## Camera

The AR tape measure, magnifier, plumb line, height finder and protractor overlay ask for the camera when you first use them. The picture is shown on your screen and isn't saved. The one exception is Photo in the AR tape measure, which saves a picture to your gallery when you tap it.

## Microphone

The sound meter asks for the microphone when you first open it. Audio is measured in short pieces in memory and thrown away at once. It's never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

## Location

The GPS speedometer uses your location to work out speed and distance, and the compass can use your approximate location to find true north. Location is used on the phone, isn't sent anywhere, and is only kept if you choose to save or share a reading.

## Physical activity

The pedometer reads Android's step counter, with your permission. Step counts stay on the phone.

## Exchange rates

The currency converter downloads public exchange rates from Frankfurter, at most once a day once you've opened it, and keeps them for offline use. The request carries nothing about you, though like any web request it shows the server your device's IP address. If you never open the converter, this never happens.

## Google Play Services for AR

This application runs on [Google Play Services for AR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.ar.core) (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

ARCore powers the AR tape measure. Google says ARCore collects performance and diagnostic data, records of how its features are used, and an account or device identifier, and that it doesn't share these with third parties. ARCore may also go online to check whether your phone supports AR, including if you never open the AR tape measure. SouMoster never receives any of this. ToolsPocket doesn't use the ARCore features that send camera video or precise location to Google.

## AI features

AI features are optional and stay off until you paste your own OpenAI API key in Settings. The key is encrypted on the phone, is sent only to OpenAI, and is left out of Android backups and device-to-device transfer. When you find a tool, describe a sum or ask for a conversion, only the words you typed go, along with the list of tools or units for it to choose from. When you tap Explain with AI on a sensor tool, the tool's name and the number on screen go. Your location, history and other readings do not. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background. It goes directly from your phone to OpenAI over an encrypted connection, and OpenAI handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Usage is billed to your OpenAI account. SouMoster never receives your key or the text. To stop, remove the key in Settings.

## Sharing and other apps

Apart from the AI features above, your data leaves ToolsPocket only when you send it: sharing a result, saving one as a note in NotesPocket, exporting a CSV, or sending an AR photo to FilePocket or another app. That app gets only what you sent, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Backups and deleting your data

Readings, calibrations, history and your OpenAI key are left out of Android backups and device-to-device transfer. Preferences may move with Android's backup. The backup in Settings is a plain file you choose where to keep. To remove the app's data, use Android Settings → Apps → ToolsPocket → Storage → Clear storage. AR photos are in your gallery, under Pictures/ToolsPocket. SouMoster holds no ToolsPocket data, so there is nothing to request from us.
