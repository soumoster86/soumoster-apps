PlayPocket is a folder-first player for the videos and music already on the phone. Playlists, favourites, subtitles and the equaliser stay with those files.

It is built to work offline. There is no account and there are no ads. The internet permission is stripped out so a library cannot quietly add one.

The app is still in development, so this page does not link to a Play listing yet.
