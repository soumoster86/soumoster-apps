NotesPocket holds notes, checklists and reminders on the phone. Labels and pictures stay with the notes they belong to.

There is no account and there are no ads. Reminders are local alarms, not messages sent through a SouMoster server. If you add your own OpenAI key, you can also summarise or tidy up a note. That stays off until you do.

The app is still in development, so there is no Play button on this page yet.
