ReadPocket is for reading, marking up and signing PDFs. A file reopens on the page you left, and highlights and notes stay beside it.

You can fill a form, merge PDFs, and write an encrypted backup that you keep. There is no account and there are no ads. If you add your own OpenAI key, you can also summarise a PDF or ask about it. That stays off until you do.

Version 0.1.0 is still in development. This page will gain a Play button when a listing exists.
