faqs:
  - q: Does BrowsePocket need an account?
    a: No. There is no account, no sync, no ads and no analytics. Your tabs, bookmarks and history stay on your phone, and SouMoster never receives them.
  - q: Which engine does it use?
    a: Android System WebView, the same engine Chrome uses, kept up to date by Google Play. Sites work as they do in Chrome, and security fixes arrive through the Play Store without an app update.
  - q: Who can see what I browse?
    a: The sites you visit, as with any browser, and your search engine when you search. DuckDuckGo is the default. Search suggestions are off until you turn them on. Safe Browsing checks pages against Google's lists of dangerous sites, and you can turn it off in Settings. SouMoster sees nothing.
  - q: How private are private tabs?
    a: They use their own cookies, storage and cache, separate from your normal tabs. Nothing from them goes into history or onto disk, and everything is deleted when the last private tab closes. Screenshots are blocked while one is on screen.
  - q: Does hiding cookie banners accept cookies?
    a: No. BrowsePocket hides the pop-up and clicks nothing, so the site never gets your consent. Third-party cookies are blocked either way.
  - q: Does BrowsePocket use AI?
    a: Only if you add your own OpenAI key in Settings. Summarise and Ask send the page's text and title, plus your question. Explain sends the text you selected and the page's title. Nothing is sent from private tabs or in the background, and SouMoster never receives the key or the text.
  - q: Can I make it my default browser?
    a: Yes. BrowsePocket offers this when you first open it, or you can choose it later in Android's default apps settings. Links from other apps then open in a new tab.

BrowsePocket is a web browser built for privacy. It blocks ads and trackers from the first page, hides cookie banners without accepting them, and keeps everything you do on your phone. It's the window to the web for the Pocket family.

There is no account and there are no ads, and a site only gets your camera, microphone or location if it asks and you allow it. Send a page to NotesPocket, open a downloaded PDF in ReadPocket, or start a search from the home-screen widget.

The app is still in development, so there is no Play button on this page yet.
