ExpensePocket is a personal ledger and wealth tracker. Income, expenses, investments, budgets, bill reminders and a month's assets stay on the phone.

There is no SouMoster account and there are no ads. A short line such as "Tea 40" is read on the phone. If you paste a Neon connection string, the ledger can sync to that database. If you paste an OpenAI key, you can ask questions about your numbers. Both stay off until you add them.

The app is still in development, so there is no Play button on this page yet.
