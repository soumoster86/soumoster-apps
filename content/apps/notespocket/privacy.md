NotesPocket is an Android app by SouMoster for notes, checklists and reminders. This is the privacy policy for the app. The website has its own policy.

## What stays on the phone

Notes, checklist items, labels, reminder times and attached images are stored on the device, unless you use the AI features described below. SouMoster does not offer an account, and the app does not show ads. The app has the internet permission only for those AI features. Without a key, it does not connect to anything.

## AI features

AI features are optional and stay off until you paste your own OpenAI API key in Settings. The key is encrypted on the phone, is sent only to OpenAI, and is left out of Android backups and device-to-device transfer. When you tap an AI action on a note, such as Summarise or Rewrite clearly, that note's words go to OpenAI. Its images and your other notes do not. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background. The text goes directly from your phone to OpenAI over an encrypted connection, and OpenAI handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Usage is billed to your OpenAI account. SouMoster never receives your key or the text. To stop, remove the key in Settings.

## Reminders

A reminder is an alarm on the phone. NotesPocket can ask to post notifications, to schedule alarms, and to restore those alarms after the phone restarts. Those alerts are local. They are not sent through a SouMoster server.

## App lock

If you turn app lock on, NotesPocket asks the phone to check your fingerprint, face or PIN. That check stays on the device.
