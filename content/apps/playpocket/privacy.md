PlayPocket is an Android app by SouMoster for playing videos and music that are already on the phone. This is the privacy policy for the app. The website has its own policy.

## What stays on the phone

Video and audio files remain where you stored them. Playlists, favourites and playback position stay on the device. SouMoster does not offer an account, and the app does not show ads. Nothing is uploaded to a SouMoster server.

## Permissions

PlayPocket asks for permission to read the videos and audio you allow. On newer versions of Android you can grant only selected videos. The app uses the usual playback permissions so audio can continue in the background and so its equaliser can adjust its own audio.

The internet permission is removed from the app, including any copy a library might try to add.
