faqs:
  - q: Why does FilePocket ask for All files access?
    a: A file manager has to see your files to manage them, and Android requires this permission for that. If you decline, you can pick individual folders instead. The trash, device-wide search and the cleaner need the full permission.
  - q: Does FilePocket need an account?
    a: No. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. SouMoster never receives your files, their names or your activity.
  - q: Why does it have the internet permission?
    a: For network drives you add yourself, and for optional AI features if you add your own OpenAI key. It connects to nothing else. Your files and login go directly between your phone and the server you chose.
  - q: Does FilePocket use AI?
    a: Only if you add your own OpenAI key in Settings. You can then summarise or explain a text file, or describe a search in plain words. Each time, only the open file's text and type, or the words you typed, go to OpenAI. File names and your other files do not. SouMoster does not receive the key or the text.
  - q: What happens if the phone restarts during a copy?
    a: The job carries on from its first unfinished item. A password-protected job fails instead, because the password is never written down.

FilePocket is a file manager for all of your phone's storage: internal, SD card and USB drives, in one place. Find a file, open it without leaving the app, and keep things tidy.

There is no account and there are no ads. Your files stay on the phone unless you share them, copy them to a network drive you added, or tap an AI action after adding your own OpenAI key. Audio and video open in PlayPocket, PDFs can go to ReadPocket, and text can be saved as a note in NotesPocket.

The app is still in development, so there is no Play button on this page yet.
