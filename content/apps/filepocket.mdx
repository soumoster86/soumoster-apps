FilePocket is a file manager for all of your phone's storage: internal, SD card and USB drives, in one place. Find a file, open it without leaving the app, and keep things tidy.

There is no account and there are no ads. Your files stay on the phone unless you share them or copy them to a network drive you added. Audio and video open in PlayPocket, PDFs can go to ReadPocket, and text can be saved as a note in NotesPocket.

The app is still in development, so there is no Play button on this page yet.
