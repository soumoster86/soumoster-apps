ExpensePocket is an Android app by SouMoster for a personal ledger and wealth tracker. This is the privacy policy for the app. The website has its own policy.

## What stays on the phone

Entries, categories, budgets, bill reminders, assets and liabilities are stored in a database on the device. SouMoster does not offer an account, and the app does not show ads.

The app does request the internet permission. It is used only for the two optional features below. Nothing is sent to a server run by SouMoster.

## Bank messages

Reading bank SMS is off until you turn it on. The app then asks for permission to read messages and to receive them. A matching message becomes a suggestion on the phone. You choose whether to save it. A notification on the lock screen says that a transaction is waiting and does not show the amount or the name.

## Sync

If you paste a Neon connection string, ExpensePocket syncs the ledger to that database when the app opens and when you leave it after a change. The string has to start with `postgresql://` or `postgres://`. It is kept in the app's private settings. Android cloud backup includes the ledger database and leaves that settings file out. A direct phone-to-phone transfer can still carry the string.

## Questions and insights

A short entry such as "Tea 40" is read on the phone. Filling an entry from a longer sentence, writing the month on Insights, and Ask your data use OpenAI, and only after you paste an API key. The key stays on the phone, is sent only to OpenAI, and is left out of Android cloud backup.

## Backup and export

You can share a copy of the phone database as a file. Import replaces the database on that phone. Savings figures are calculated when you look at them. They are not stored as their own record.

## App lock

If you turn app lock on, ExpensePocket asks the phone to check your fingerprint or PIN. That check stays on the device. While the lock is on, the app also asks Android to keep its contents out of screenshots.
