SouMoster is an independent studio. The games live on the [games site](https://sou-moster.vercel.app). The Pocket apps are the other half: small Android tools for documents, media, notes and reading.

Each app is meant to work without an account and without ads. What you scan, play, write or read stays on the phone. The same line is on every lock screen: Designed with love by SouMoster.

A new app joins this site by adding one content file. The pages, the grid and the privacy URL follow from that file, so the layout does not have to change.

These pages are the public face of the apps, including the privacy policy a Google Play listing can point at. An app's badge says whether it is live, in a closed test, still in development, or only announced.
