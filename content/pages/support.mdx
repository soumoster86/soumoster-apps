## How to report a bug

1. Note the app name and the version printed on its lock screen.
2. Write what you expected and what happened instead.
3. Email [soumoster@gmail.com](mailto:soumoster@gmail.com). A screenshot of the screen helps. Do not send documents, photos, passwords or the contents of a note.

There is no account to reset, and there is no in-app inbox. Email is the whole support desk.
