This policy covers the SouMoster Apps website. Each Android app has its own policy, linked from that app's page. Those are the URLs a Google Play listing should use.

## What this site collects

The site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no comments and no forms that store what you type. Contact is a mailto link, so a message goes to email on your device.

Page views are counted with Vercel Web Analytics. That count is cookieless. The site does not load advertising, and it does not set a cookie for consent because it does not need one.

## What stays in the browser

If you switch to the light theme, the choice is saved in local storage on your browser. That is not a cookie, and it is not sent to SouMoster as an account setting.

Fonts are self-hosted with the site. They are not loaded from a third-party font service.
